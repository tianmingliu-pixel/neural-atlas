神经图谱Neural Atlas

中央是一台 AI 核心。提问后，它会从 21 种成熟神经网络里挑出最匹配的一种换成自己的大脑，在颅内逐层推理、迭代修正，把思考过程显现出来。At the center sits an AI core. Ask a question and it picks the best-matching of 21 mature neural networks, swaps it in as its brain, reasons layer by layer inside the skull, refines itself, and lets you watch the thinking surface.

两种回答是怎么做出来的How the two answers are made

同一个问题走两条路。左边一次写完；右边先把「思考」外化成结构化中间表示，选一种网络结构作推理模板，自我修正 3 轮，再带着这些约束作答。The same question takes two paths. A writes the answer in one go. B first externalizes its thinking as a structured intermediate representation, picks a network architecture as its reasoning template, critiques itself three times, and then answers under those constraints.

  • 把思考外化。第一次调用不写答案，只产出结构化 JSON。这相当于把 chain-of-thought 变成可检查、可可视化的中间表示，3D 里大脑的每一步都直接来自这份 JSON。Externalize the thinking. The first call writes no answer, only structured JSON. This turns chain-of-thought into an intermediate representation you can inspect and visualize; every step the 3D brain shows comes straight from that JSON.
    • 
        
  • 架构路由就是归纳偏置。模型要从 21 种网络里选一个和问题结构同构的作推理模板：全局关系选注意力，空间分解选卷积，实体关系选图网络，序贯决策选 DQN，由粗到精选扩散。模板决定了问题被怎样拆开。Architecture routing is an inductive bias. The model picks, from 21 networks, the one whose structure mirrors the question: attention for global relations, convolution for spatial decomposition, GNNs for entity relations, DQN for sequential decisions, diffusion for coarse-to-fine refinement. The template decides how the question gets taken apart.
    • 
        
  • 逐层分解。5 个步骤按所选网络的层顺序展开（输入 → … → 输出），思路接近 plan-and-solve、least-to-most 这类分步求解法。Layered decomposition. Five steps unfold in the chosen network's layer order (input → … → output), close to stepwise methods such as plan-and-solve and least-to-most.
    • 
        
  • 3 轮自我修正。每轮写出草稿推理的一个具体缺陷和修正方案（Self-Refine / Reflexion 的做法），全部作为硬约束注入第二次调用。Three self-refinement rounds. Each round names one concrete flaw in the draft reasoning and its fix (the Self-Refine / Reflexion approach). All of them are injected into the second call as hard constraints.
    • 
        
  • 条件生成。第二次调用用 default 档，模型先在内部思考，再按推理阶段组织结构、吸收每一条修正写出答案。Conditioned generation. The second call uses the default tier: the model thinks internally first, then writes an answer organized by the reasoning stages that absorbs every fix.
    • 
        
  • 边界要清楚。这里的「迭代」发生在语言层（提示与上下文），没有梯度下降，也不改任何网络权重；21 种网络是推理模板和可视化映射，并没有在浏览器里运行。代价是更多调用、更高延迟、更多 token。Know the limits. The iteration happens in language (prompt and context): no gradient descent, no weight updates. The 21 networks are reasoning templates and a visual mapping; none of them runs in the browser. The cost is more calls, more latency and more tokens.
